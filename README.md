# Super Epic Cool Awesome Stuff: The Bar Game
A game made for the LBS gymnasium game-jam #3 (2026).\
(Duration: 42 hours. Theme: "Riding Farmers")

> [!NOTE]
> Things may be too easy, too hard, and/or confusing. \
> Expect a buggy mess (because game-jam).

## How to play
Download the source and open it in Godot (was developed in 4.6.2).

The goal is to keep the farmer on the mechanical bull by dragging their limbs to the handles
when the farmer loses grip.\
Lose grip on all 4 limbs = die.
